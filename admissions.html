Admissions

We welcome students who are ready to learn, grow and participate actively in our school community.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed admission application form
  • Previous school report
  • Birth certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Transfer documents where applicable

Admission Process

  1. Obtain an application form.
  2. Complete and submit the application.
  3. Attend the admission interview where applicable.
  4. Receive the admission decision.
  5. Complete student registration.

Need More Information?

Contact the school administration for information about available spaces, fees and admission dates.
